# Renewal Management Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook standardizes how Fly GACA forecasts, prepares for, and executes
contract renewals so the team sustains a **renewal rate > 95%**. Renewals are
treated as the natural outcome of demonstrated compliance value, not a
last-minute negotiation.

## Renewal Forecasting

Each renewal is assigned a forecast category based on health and engagement:

| Forecast | Criteria | Action |
|----------|----------|--------|
| Commit | Healthy band, champion engaged, value realized | Confirm and process early |
| Likely | Neutral band, no major risks | Reinforce value, close gaps |
| At-Risk | At-Risk band or open escalation | Trigger [at-risk playbook](at-risk-playbook.md) |

Forecasts are reviewed in the weekly CS pipeline meeting and updated as the
health score recalculates.

## Renewal Timeline

| Days to renewal | Activity |
|-----------------|----------|
| T-120 | Identify renewal, confirm forecast, review health & usage |
| T-90 | Hold value review / QBR; surface expansion opportunities |
| T-60 | Deliver renewal proposal; align stakeholders |
| T-30 | Finalize terms, secure approvals, handle objections |
| T-0 | Execute contract; confirm continuity of service |
| T+30 | Renewal retro; reset success plan for the new term |

## Value Reinforcement

Quantify the compliance outcomes delivered during the term:
- GACA audits passed and findings avoided.
- Compliance deadlines met on time via the platform.
- Audit-preparation and document-management hours saved.
- Adoption growth across core modules.

Pull this evidence from the QBR record — see
[`docs/qbr-template.md`](qbr-template.md).

## Negotiation Strategy

- Lead with realized value and outcomes, not features or discounts.
- Defend price by tying it to compliance risk reduction (cost of a failed audit
  or grounded operation vs. subscription cost).
- Offer multi-year terms or tier upgrades in exchange for commitment.
- Hold a clear concession ladder; protect margin and avoid setting precedents.

## Risk Mitigation

- Any account in the At-Risk forecast enters the
  [at-risk playbook](at-risk-playbook.md) immediately.
- Confirm budget owner and procurement timeline early to avoid lapse.
- Map all decision-makers: accountable manager, compliance officer, finance.

## Multi-Year Planning

- Propose 2–3 year agreements for Professional/Enterprise accounts with strong
  health and stable sponsorship.
- Build in expansion checkpoints aligned to the customer's fleet/operation
  growth and evolving GACA obligations.

## Stakeholder Alignment

| Role | Interest | Engagement |
|------|----------|------------|
| Accountable Manager | Regulatory accountability, risk | Executive value review |
| Compliance Officer | Day-to-day compliance outcomes | Usage & outcomes review |
| Finance / Procurement | Cost, terms, ROI | Proposal & commercial terms |

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Renewal rate | > 95% |
| On-time renewals (no lapse) | > 90% |
| Multi-year contract share | Growing |
| Net revenue retention | > 100% |
