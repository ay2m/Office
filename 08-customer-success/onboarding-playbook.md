# Onboarding Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook defines how Fly GACA onboards a new B2B customer (flight school /
ATO, charter operator, MRO, or commercial operator) from signed contract to
first realized value. The goal is fast time-to-value, strong early adoption
(> 80% of licensed seats active), and a healthy first health score within 90
days.

## Success Criteria

| Milestone | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| Kickoff held | Within 5 business days of signature |
| Admin + first cohort provisioned | Day 14 |
| Adoption (active vs. licensed seats) | > 60% by Day 30, > 80% by Day 90 |
| First value milestone reached | Day 60 |
| First health score in Healthy/Neutral band | Day 90 |

## Stakeholder Mapping

| Role | Interest |
|------|----------|
| Sponsor (Head of Training / Ops) | Outcomes, ROI, accountability |
| Admin / champion | Day-to-day rollout, seat management |
| End users (cadets, pilots, instructors, technicians) | Study, tools, library, currency |

## 30 / 60 / 90-Day Plan

### Days 0–30 — Launch
- Kickoff call: confirm goals, success criteria, and timeline.
- Provision the account; map seats to cohorts/roles.
- Admin enablement + first end-user training session.
- Configure currency tracking and relevant library/tool access.
- Establish the communication cadence and escalation path.

### Days 31–60 — Adopt
- Drive adoption to target across the first cohort.
- Run a tools/study workshop; share best practices and use cases.
- Review early usage; close adoption gaps proactively.
- Confirm the first value milestone (e.g., cohort study progress, exam prep).

### Days 61–90 — Realize
- Demonstrate value against the agreed success criteria.
- Compute the first [health score](customer-health-scoring.md).
- Transition to the steady-state CS cadence (QBRs, ongoing check-ins).
- Identify early expansion signals (see [expansion playbook](expansion-playbook.md)).
