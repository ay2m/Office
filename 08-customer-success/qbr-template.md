# Quarterly Business Review (QBR) Template

## Purpose

A QBR is a structured executive checkpoint that reinforces value, aligns on
goals, and surfaces renewal and expansion opportunities. Run QBRs quarterly for
Professional and Enterprise accounts (and high-potential Starter accounts).

Copy this template per account/quarter and complete each section before the
meeting.
