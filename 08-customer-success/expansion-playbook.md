# Expansion / Upsell Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook describes how Fly GACA identifies and executes expansion — seat
growth, tier upgrades, and cross-sell of additional modules — to drive net
revenue retention above 100% while deepening each customer's compliance
coverage. Expansion is pursued primarily with **Healthy-band** accounts that
have already realized value.

## Growth-Opportunity Identification

Watch for expansion signals:

| Signal | Opportunity |
|--------|-------------|
| Adoption near or above licensed seats | Seat expansion |
| Strong usage but limited to core modules | Cross-sell adjacent modules |
| New fleet, base, or operation type | Scope expansion |
| Repeated requests for features in a higher tier | Tier upgrade |
| New GACA obligation affecting the customer | Compliance module cross-sell |
| Healthy score + engaged champion | Advocacy + expansion candidate |

## Tier-Upgrade Paths

| From | To | Typical trigger |
|------|----|-----------------|
| Starter | Professional | More users, multi-module compliance needs |
| Professional | Enterprise | Multiple operations, advanced audit/reporting, SSO |
| Add-on modules | Any tier | Cross-sell of document automation, analytics, training |

Map upgrades to the customer segments in
[`customer-success.md`](../customer-success.md) (flight schools, charter
operators, MROs, commercial operators).

## Cross-Sell Modules

- Advanced audit-preparation and finding-management.
- Document automation and controlled-document management.
- Compliance analytics and executive dashboards.
- Training / certification tracking for crew and maintenance staff.

## Business-Case Development

1. Quantify current value realized (audits passed, hours saved, findings
   avoided).
2. Identify the gap the expansion closes (new obligation, scale, or capability).
3. Model the ROI for the customer in their own compliance/operational terms.
4. Align the proposal to an upcoming milestone (QBR, renewal, audit cycle).

## Pricing Discussion

- Anchor on incremental value, not list price.
- Bundle expansion with renewal where timing aligns (see
  [renewal playbook](renewal-playbook.md)).
- Offer ramped pricing for large seat or multi-operation rollouts.

## Expansion Tracking & Revenue Attribution

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Net revenue retention | > 100% |
| Accounts with an open expansion opportunity | Growing |
| Expansion win rate | > 30% |
| Expansion revenue attributed to CS | Tracked per quarter |

Log every expansion in the CRM with source signal, owner, stage, and amount so
revenue can be attributed back to the CS motion.
