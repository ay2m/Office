# Voice of Customer (VoC) Program

## Purpose

The Voice-of-Customer program is how FlyGACA systematically collects, analyzes,
and acts on customer feedback. It feeds the Sentiment dimension of the health
score and sustains the targets of **NPS > 50** and **CSAT > 90%**.

## Survey Program

| Survey | Measures | Scale |
|--------|----------|-------|
| NPS | Loyalty / advocacy | 0–10 (Promoter 9–10, Passive 7–8, Detractor 0–6) |
| CSAT | Satisfaction with an interaction | 1–5 |
| CES | Effort to get value / resolve an issue | 1–7 |

## Cadence & Triggers

| Survey | When |
|--------|------|
| NPS | Relationship survey every 6 months; on milestone (post-onboarding, post-renewal) |
| CSAT | Transactional — after support ticket closure and after training sessions |
| CES | After onboarding completion and after major feature adoption |

## Survey Templates

**NPS:** "How likely are you to recommend FlyGACA to another aviation operator?
(0–10)" + "What is the primary reason for your score?"

**CSAT:** "How satisfied were you with this interaction? (1–5)" + optional
comment.

**CES:** "How easy was it to achieve your goal with FlyGACA? (1–7)" + optional
comment.

## Segmentation

Analyze results by customer segment (flight schools, charter operators, MROs,
commercial operators), plan tier, and health band so themes can be tied to the
right playbook and persona (accountable manager, compliance officer, end user).

## Analysis

- Track NPS trend and Promoter/Passive/Detractor mix.
- Tag verbatim feedback into themes (value, adoption, support, product gaps,
  compliance needs).
- Correlate sentiment with the health score and renewal/expansion outcomes.
- Report results in the monthly CS review and in account QBRs.

## Close-the-Loop Process

| Respondent | Action | Owner | SLA |
|------------|--------|-------|-----|
| Detractor (NPS 0–6) | Personal follow-up; route to [at-risk playbook](at-risk-playbook.md) | CSM | < 2 business days |
| Passive (NPS 7–8) | Identify the gap to a 9–10 | CSM | < 1 week |
| Promoter (NPS 9–10) | Invite to advocacy / reference program | CSM | < 1 week |

Every product request captured is logged and routed to the product team, and the
customer is updated when the loop is closed.

## Targets

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| NPS | > 50 |
| CSAT | > 90% |
| Survey response rate | > 30% |
| Detractor follow-up within SLA | 100% |
