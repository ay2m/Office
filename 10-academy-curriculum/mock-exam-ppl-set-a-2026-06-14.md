## Rationale Notes for Instructors / Self-Study

**Q4 (VFR minima, Class E):** GACAR Part 91 mirrors ICAO Annex 2 for Class E. The 500 ft / 1,000 ft / 2,000 ft values differ from Class G (below 1,200 ft AGL: 1 SM and clear of cloud). Verify current GACAR Part 91 §91.165 for the exact Saudi minima table.

**Q16 (Saudi weather):** This question targets Saudi-specific meteorological hazards not addressed in generic ICAO questions. Blowing dust (BLDU) and blowing sand (BLSA) are common METAR codes at inland Saudi stations and can reduce visibility to below 800 m with little warning. Check Saudi AIP MET for current observational reporting.

**Q21 (Map scale):** A 1:500,000 chart is the standard VFR en-route scale. 1 mm on paper = 500 m on ground = 0.27 nm approximately. 1 cm = 5 km = 2.7 nm.

**Q22 (Wind triangle):** A wind from 360° (true north) on a track of 270° creates a full 90° crosswind from the right (from the north, relative to a west-bound aircraft). The drift is to the south (left). To maintain a 270° track, the pilot turns left — a right wind correction angle. WCA = arcsin(Vw/TAS) = arcsin(20/110) ≈ 10°. Heading ≈ 260° (M). *Verify with an E6B or aviation calculator.*

**Q31 (Load factor 60° bank):** LF = 1/cos(θ). cos(60°) = 0.5. LF = 2.0 G. Stall speed in a 60° bank = Vs × √2 ≈ 1.41 × straight-and-level Vs.

**Q36 (Transponder codes):** 7700 = Emergency; 7600 = Communication failure (lost comms); 7500 = Unlawful interference (hijack). These codes must be memorised; never select them inadvertently.

**Q39 (Light-gun signals):** For aircraft on the ground: flashing red = taxi clear of landing area. For aircraft in flight: flashing red = aerodrome unsafe, do not land. Steady red (ground) = stop; steady green (ground) = clear to take off.
