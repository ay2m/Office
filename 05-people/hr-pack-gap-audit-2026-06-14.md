## 5. Bilingual Coverage Note

Current 05-people documents are English-primary. For Saudi national employees (Saudization compliance), Arabic versions of the employment contract, grievance procedure, disciplinary notices, and termination letters are strongly recommended and may be required by HRSD in any dispute. The two templates drafted in this pack (Offer Letter and Leave & PTO Policy) are bilingual-structured with EN primary and AR section headers.
