## Gaps, Issues, and Recommendations

### GAP-1 — 06-product-eng physical location is split (HIGH)
The `06-product-eng/` top-level bucket folder contains only an empty `diagrams/` subfolder. All 25 product/engineering documents actually live in:
- `library/06-product-eng/` (canonical copies)
- `flygaca/office/` (working copies in the code repo, partially duplicated)

This creates a risk of divergence and confusion. The `README.md` describes `06-product-eng/` as the home, but that is not where the files are.

**Recommendation:** Move all files from `library/06-product-eng/` into `06-product-eng/` and reconcile duplicates in `flygaca/office/` (keep symlinks or a reference, not full copies). The `library/` folder should contain regulatory corpus only, not engineering docs.

### GAP-2 — 06-product-eng: missing spec-currency-tracker.md (MEDIUM)
The currency tracker (Psalms of Currency in the Book; central retention feature per the action plan) has no dedicated product spec. There are specs for the CRM, instructor dashboard, and freshness pipeline, but not for the feature the action plan calls the single most important post-exam retention mechanism.

**Recommendation:** Add `spec-currency-tracker.md` to the 06-product-eng bucket.

### GAP-3 — 06-product-eng: missing spec-captain-adel-refusal-protocol.md (MEDIUM)
The action plan identifies the AI hallucination risk as existential and defines a specific refusal/fallback protocol ("refuse and redirect when retrieval confidence is low; surface retrieved snippet, not just citation link"). This is not codified as a spec, only referenced in the action plan and partially in Book V.

**Recommendation:** Add `spec-captain-adel-refusal-protocol.md` encoding the exact fallback behaviour, confidence threshold approach, and what "redirect to GACA" looks like in UI.

### GAP-4 — 09-investor-relations: no pitch deck (MEDIUM)
The investor relations bucket has a DDQ, FAQ, update template, and target list — but no pitch deck. For a platform at pre-seed that is actively seeking investment, the deck is typically the primary IR artefact.

**Recommendation:** Add `Pitch Deck.pdf` (or a link to a Canva/Slides source). Even a placeholder stub is better than the gap.
