Foundation · v1.0
01

Principles

Five rules that turn the logo into a system
01

Night sky as canvas

Dark surfaces are primary, not "dark mode." Light surfaces are for reading dense regulatory text.

02

Two confident strokes

Hierarchy comes from a few strong elements, not many small ones. Avoid clutter.

03

Lift, not lecture

Tone is precise but never stuffy. The surface should feel modern and inviting.

04

Heritage without pastiche

Reference Saudi visual heritage sparingly — through color and proportion, not ornament.

05

Bilingual by default

Latin and Arabic must coexist. Type, spacing, and direction support both equally.
