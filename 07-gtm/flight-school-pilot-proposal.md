# Flight Academy Enterprise Pilot Program Proposal

### Commercial Pilot Program & Syllabus Integration for Saudi Flight Training Academies (GACAR Part 141 ATOs)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

As Saudi Arabia accelerates the **National Aviation Strategy 2030** to train over 20,000 aviation professionals, modern Approved Training Organizations (ATOs) face a common challenge: ground school attrition and cadet friction with complex regulatory exams (GACAR Air Law, Saudi AIP, Meteorology, Instrument Procedures).

**Fly GACA for Flight Schools** provides an end-to-end digital ground-school and regulatory enablement platform:
1. **Interactive Regulatory Ground Training**: Full bilingual GACAR corpus with cited question banks, flashcards, and timed mock checkride exams.
2. **AI Instructor (Captain Adel)**: 24/7 grounded tutor that explains questions citing exact GACAR sections and AIP chapters.
3. **Flight School Admin Dashboard**: Real-time cohort health score, weak-area analytics, and stage-check readiness tracking.

## 3. Flight School Admin Dashboard & Analytics

The dedicated **School Admin Dashboard** empowers Chief Flight Instructors (CFIs) and Ground Instructors:

- **Cohort Readiness Tracking**: Automated readiness threshold (≥75% pass mark) determining when a cadet is prepared for official GACA written exams.
- **Weak Subject Diagnostics**: Aggregate performance metrics identifying cohort bottlenecks (e.g. Altimeter setting procedures, Wake turbulence separation).
- **Stage Check Endorsements**: Audit-ready progress logs verifying ground school completion prior to practical flight checks.
- **PDPL Compliant**: Full compliance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law with granular consent controls.
