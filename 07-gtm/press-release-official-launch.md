**RIYADH, SAUDI ARABIA — September 1, 2026** — **BDA Company International** (شركة بدع الدولية), a Saudi limited liability enterprise registered in Riyadh, today announced the official commercial launch of **Fly GACA** ([flygaca.com](https://flygaca.com)) and **Captain Adel** ([captadel.com](https://captadel.com)) — the Kingdom’s first comprehensive, independent digital regulatory library, Electronic Flight Bag (EFB), and Artificial Intelligence flight instructor platform built specifically for Saudi civil aviation.

As the Kingdom accelerates its **National Aviation Strategy 2030**—aiming to quadruple air passenger transit, expand the civil aircraft fleet, and train more than 20,000 aviation professionals—Fly GACA bridges a critical operational gap: providing student pilots, certified flight instructors (CFIs), airlines, and Approved Training Organizations (ATOs) with instantaneous, indexed access to civil aviation rules, flight tools, and AI-powered ground instruction.

---

## 🚀 Key Pillars of the Fly GACA Ecosystem

1. **The Open GACAR Library (74 Parts):** Complete digital indexing of all 74 General Authority of Civil Aviation Regulations (GACAR) Parts, including Part 61 (Pilot Certification), Part 67 (Medical), Part 91 (General Operating Rules), Part 121 (Commercial Transport), Part 135 (On-Demand Operations), and Part 141 (Pilot Schools). Every paragraph, subpart, and appendix is deep-linked for instant verification.
2. **55+ Flight Deck Calculators & AIP Reference:** A comprehensive suite of mathematical calculators—crosswind and headwind resolvers, density altitude, weight and balance CG envelope analyzers, and Saudi aerodrome directories—working 100% offline in native iOS/iPadOS Electronic Flight Bag (EFB) mode.
3. **Captain Adel (كابتن عادل) — AI Flight Instructor:** Powered by advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and trained with strict "Cite-or-Refuse" precision. Captain Adel answers complex operational, regulatory, and airspace questions by quoting exact GACAR chapter and verse, eliminating hallucinations and supporting both Arabic and English.
4. **Academy Enablement & Ground School (Part 141):** Over 1,000 curated exam questions, Leitner Spaced-Repetition flashcards, and a dedicated Chief Flight Instructor (CFI) portal providing cohort readiness diagnostics and stage-check endorsements.

## 🔒 Enterprise Compliance & Data Sovereignty

Operating under Saudi law, Fly GACA is fully compliant with the **Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)**, keeping all student records and institutional analytics secure. Commercial subscriptions for flight academies are integrated with **ZATCA Phase 2 (Fatoora)** e-invoicing, providing automated tax clearance and instant onboarding for corporate entities.
