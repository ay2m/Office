## 1. Founder Summary Letter  *(draft — rewrite in your own voice, 400–600 words)*

**The most important thing this quarter:** we are drawing a hard line under *building* and
turning the whole company toward *existing and earning*. The product is done. What is not
done is the company.

**What we said vs. what we did.** We set out to complete the product across eleven phases —
and we did: the GACAR library, Captain Adel answering with cited sources, a 162-question
study system, 21 tools, eleven guides, full Arabic RTL, offline support, and a launch-ready
SEO pass, all deployed and live. By any build measure we are a year ahead. The honest
counterpoint: in the same period we did **not** engage the IP lawyer, did **not** register
the legal entity, and have **not** signed a single customer. The most recent work was Arabic
localization and a repository reorganization — more building, when the binding constraint had
already shifted to legal existence and demand.

**What did not go as planned, and why.** The legal/entity track kept losing to the build
track. Building is solo, controllable, and never says no; a lawyer's bill, a committed legal
name, and a sales rejection are none of those. That is precisely why they are the work that
remains. The product roadmap itself concluded months ago that "the legal/entity track is now
the critical path — the product itself is built." We are now acting on our own conclusion.

**What keeps me up at night (top 2 risks).** (1) **The name.** "Fly GACA" may be
unregistrable as a trademark and carries passing-off exposure because it leans on a
government authority's identity; every week we build under it raises the cost of a forced
rebrand. (2) **The clock.** The new Saudi Copyright Law takes effect **12 August 2026**, with
implementing regulations on the official-documents exclusion and an AI-training exception —
both central to Captain Adel's corpus rights. The lawyer opinion must reflect the new law, so
the engagement cannot wait.

**What I need from the board this quarter.** Endorse the reprioritization (DEC-006):
back the freeze on new build and the focus on lawyer → name → entity → first customer. Hold
me to the two scoreboard numbers — **does the company legally exist, and how many customers
have paid** — and to the Sprint-0 commitment that the lawyer brief and the first three school
outreach emails go out **this week**.
