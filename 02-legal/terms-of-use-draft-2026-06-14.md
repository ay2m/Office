## 2. Educational Purpose — Independent Status — Not for Operational Use

### 2.1 Educational Platform Only

The Service is provided solely for **education, study, reference, and familiarisation** with Saudi civil aviation regulations and related material. It is not an operational tool.

**You must not rely on the Service for:**
- Flight planning, in-flight decisions, or pre-flight briefing
- Aircraft dispatch or airworthiness determinations
- Aircraft maintenance sign-off
- Any regulatory compliance determination
- Any other operational aviation purpose

### 2.2 GACA Is the Authoritative Source

The General Authority of Civil Aviation (GACA) is the sole authoritative source for Saudi civil aviation regulations, aeronautical information, and official publications. Where Fly GACA references, describes, indexes, or reproduces any regulatory text, the official document published by GACA at [gaca.gov.sa](https://www.gaca.gov.sa) always prevails. **Always verify against the latest official GACA publication before any professional or operational reliance.**

### 2.3 AIRAC-Cycle Material

Material derived from the Aeronautical Information Publication (AIP), aerodromes, charts, or Notice to Airmen (NOTAM) information changes on the 28-day AIRAC cycle and can change between cycles. Any such material on the Service is provided for **familiarisation only**. It is not current operational data. Always obtain current operational aeronautical information from the official sources.

### 2.4 Captain Adel AI — Additional Limitations

The Captain Adel AI study assistant is an educational tool that may contain errors, omissions, hallucinations, or outdated information. Its outputs are study aids, not authoritative interpretations. Captain Adel cites sources and links to official GACA documents, but its responses must never be treated as final regulatory determinations. See Section 7 for AI-specific terms.
