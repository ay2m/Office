## 1. Independent Educational Platform

Fly GACA is a **privately built and operated educational reference platform**. It is designed to help pilots, cadets, flight instructors, and aviation professionals in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia study Saudi civil aviation regulations and stay current with regulatory changes.

Fly GACA is **not**:
- An official publication of, or authorised by, the General Authority of Civil Aviation (GACA)
- A service of, sponsored by, or connected to any department or agency of the Government of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia
- An alternative to or replacement for official GACA publications, the GACA website, or official aeronautical information
- A source of operational aviation data

The use of "GACA" and "GACAR" throughout this platform is **purely descriptive** — referring to the General Authority of Civil Aviation and its published regulations as the subject matter that this platform helps users study. This use does not imply any affiliation with, authorisation from, or endorsement by GACA.

---

## 2. GACA Is the Authoritative Source

Regulations, aeronautical information publications, forms, directives, and all other official aviation documents are published by the General Authority of Civil Aviation. Those documents are the authoritative and legally binding texts.

Where Fly GACA references, summarises, indexes, or reproduces any regulatory text or official document:
- It does so for **educational and study purposes only**
- The official copy published by GACA **always prevails**
- If anything on this platform conflicts with the official GACA publication, the official publication is correct and this platform is not
- Regulatory texts may have been amended since they were indexed on this platform; always confirm the currency of any document before reliance

## 3. For Study and Reference Only — Not for Operational Use

**The content on this platform must not be used for any operational aviation purpose.**

Uses that are **not permitted**:
- Flight planning or pre-flight briefing
- In-flight decision-making
- Aircraft dispatch or airworthiness determinations
- Maintenance sign-off or continuing airworthiness
- Air traffic management
- Any regulatory compliance determination with legal or operational effect

The platform is intended only for personal study, examination preparation, and regulatory familiarisation. Any professional or operational action must be based on current, official documentation from GACA and other competent authorities.

---

## 4. Aeronautical Information — AIRAC Cycle Warning

Material on this platform that is derived from or references the Aeronautical Information Publication (AIP), aerodrome information, VFR charts, instrument approach procedures, or airspace descriptions is provided **for familiarisation only**.

Such material:
- Changes on the 28-day AIRAC cycle
- May change between AIRAC cycles (SUP, AIC, NOTAM)
- Is NOT current operational aeronautical information
- Is NOT a substitute for official pre-flight documentation

**Before any flight, always obtain current aeronautical information from the official sources, including current NOTAMs, ATIS, and the current effective AIP from GACA.**

## 7. Captain Adel — AI Study Assistant

Captain Adel is an AI-powered educational study assistant. It is designed to help users understand and study Saudi civil aviation regulations. Users should be aware of the following:

**7.1 Educational Tool Only**
Captain Adel's outputs are study aids. They are not authoritative regulatory interpretations, legal opinions, or operational guidance.

**7.2 AI Limitations**
AI language models can produce:
- Factual errors ("hallucinations")
- Outdated information that does not reflect recent regulatory amendments
- Incomplete or oversimplified summaries of complex regulatory provisions
- Confident-sounding responses that are nonetheless incorrect

**7.3 Source Verification**
Captain Adel cites its sources and links to official GACA documents wherever possible. Users must independently verify all answers against the official source before any professional or operational reliance.

**7.4 No Reproductive of Restricted Content**
Captain Adel is configured not to reproduce ICAO Annexes, aircraft Pilot's Operating Handbooks (POH), Approved Flight Manuals (AFM), or other documents that are the proprietary intellectual property of their respective owners.

**7.5 Reporting Errors**
If you believe Captain Adel has provided materially incorrect information about a regulation, please report it at support@flygaca.com.
