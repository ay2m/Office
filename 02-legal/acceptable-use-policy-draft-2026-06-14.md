## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") sets out the rules governing your use of:

- The Fly GACA website at **flygaca.com** and all sub-domains
- The Fly GACA PWA and Android application
- The **Captain Adel** AI study assistant
- The Fly GACA **Library** (regulatory document search and reader)
- The **Academy Dashboard** (B2B flight school and training organisation features)
- All related APIs, tools, and content

This AUP applies to:
- Visitors browsing the website without an account
- Individual subscribers (Free, Pro tier)
- Academy operators and their provisioned cadets (Academy tier)
- API users, integration partners, and developers

This AUP is incorporated into and forms part of:
- The **Fly GACA Terms of Use** (for public/anonymous visitors and Free tier)
- The **Fly GACA Customer Agreement & EULA** (for paid subscribers and Academy operators)

In the event of conflict between this AUP and the Customer Agreement, the Customer Agreement prevails for paid subscribers.

---

## 2. The Educational Purpose of Fly GACA

Fly GACA is an **independent educational reference platform** for Saudi civil aviation. Its sole purpose is to help pilots, cadets, flight instructors, and aviation professionals study and stay current with Saudi civil aviation regulations.

**Fly GACA is not:**
- An official source of aviation regulations
- Affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the General Authority of Civil Aviation (GACA) or any Saudi government body
- A substitute for official GACA publications, aircraft manuals, or qualified professional advice
- A platform for operational aviation decisions, flight planning, dispatch, or maintenance sign-off

Everything in this AUP is intended to protect the educational integrity of the platform, the safety of aviation professionals who use it, and the reputation Fly GACA must maintain as a trusted independent reference.

## 3. Permitted Uses

You may use Fly GACA for:

3.1 **Personal educational study** — studying Saudi civil aviation regulations (GACAR, AIP, advisory circulars) for the purpose of preparing for GACA licence examinations, ratings, recurrency checks, or general professional development.

3.2 **Reference and citation** — referring to and citing GACA regulatory content for educational discussion, study notes, or academic research, provided that you always cite the official GACA source and make clear the material originated from GACA, not from Fly GACA.

3.3 **Academy cadet training** — Academy-tier use by GACA-approved Part 141/142 training organisations and their enrolled cadets, for the purposes described in the Order Form and the Customer Agreement.

3.4 **Captain Adel study queries** — asking the AI study assistant questions about Saudi civil aviation regulations for the purpose of understanding regulatory content, preparing for examinations, or exploring related study topics.

3.5 **Sharing study insights** — sharing platform-generated summaries or study notes within your personal study group, provided this is non-commercial and the educational, non-official nature of the content is preserved.

## 4. Prohibited Uses

The following uses are strictly prohibited. They apply to all users including Free-tier visitors, Pro subscribers, Academy operators, and cadets.

### 4.1 Safety-Critical and Operational Misuse

4.1.1 You must **not** use Fly GACA content, Captain Adel responses, or any material from the platform to make **operational aviation decisions**, including but not limited to: flight planning, in-flight decisions, dispatch, airworthiness assessments, maintenance sign-off, or ATC coordination.

4.1.2 You must **not** present Captain Adel responses as **authoritative regulatory guidance** to third parties, or rely on them as a substitute for current, official GACA publications.

4.1.3 You must **not** reproduce or distribute Fly GACA content in any context where a reader might reasonably treat it as an authoritative, current, or official regulatory source.

4.1.4 You must **not** use aeronautical information from the platform (AIP-derived material, aerodrome charts, frequencies) for **navigation, flight planning, or any operational purpose**. This material changes on the 28-day AIRAC cycle and is displayed for familiarisation only.

### 4.2 Impersonation and Misrepresentation

4.2.1 You must **not** represent Fly GACA as an official GACA service, government platform, or as affiliated with any Saudi government authority.

4.2.2 You must **not** remove, obscure, alter, or circumvent any disclaimer, watermark, attribution notice, or "not for operational use" warning displayed on the platform.

4.2.3 You must **not** impersonate GACA, any other government authority, Fly GACA, the founder, or another user.

4.2.4 You must **not** create or distribute modified versions of Fly GACA content that remove the independent/unofficial attribution.

### 4.3 Technical Misuse

4.3.1 You must **not** scrape, crawl, harvest, or bulk-extract content from the platform by automated means (including bots, spiders, scripts, or browser automation) without prior written permission from Fly GACA.

4.3.2 You must **not** probe, scan, test, or attempt to exploit vulnerabilities in the platform's infrastructure, authentication systems, or APIs.

4.3.3 You must **not** attempt to circumvent, disable, or bypass any security control, rate limit, access restriction, or content protection mechanism.

4.3.4 You must **not** attempt to access another user's account, data, or the Captain Adel chat history of any other user.

4.3.5 You must **not** reverse-engineer, decompile, disassemble, or attempt to derive the source code of the Fly GACA application or the Captain Adel AI model.

4.3.6 You must **not** use the platform in a way that places an unreasonable or disproportionate load on the infrastructure (e.g., automated bulk queries to Captain Adel).

### 4.4 Prompt Injection and AI Misuse

4.4.1 You must **not** submit prompts to Captain Adel designed to: bypass the system instructions; extract confidential system context; cause the AI to impersonate a GACA official or authoritative source; or generate content outside the aviation educational scope of the platform.

4.4.2 You must **not** use Captain Adel to generate content that you intend to publish, sell, or distribute as your own original work without disclosing that it was AI-generated.

4.4.3 You must **not** include **personal data of third parties** in Captain Adel queries (names, national ID numbers, passport numbers, medical information, or any data that would identify another individual) without their consent.

4.4.4 You must **not** use Captain Adel to generate, distribute, or amplify **misinformation about aviation safety, regulations, or operational procedures**.

### 4.5 Commercial Misuse

4.5.1 You must **not** resell, sublicence, or provide access to the platform to third parties without a signed White-Label or Reseller Agreement with Fly GACA.

4.5.2 You must **not** commercially reproduce, republish, or distribute Fly GACA's original educational content (study notes, explanations, exam questions authored by Fly GACA) without a written licence.

4.5.3 You must **not** use the platform to train, fine-tune, or create derivative AI/ML models without prior written permission.

4.5.4 You must **not** use data from the platform to build a competing product or service.

### 4.6 Unlawful and Harmful Content

4.6.1 You must **not** use the platform for any purpose that is unlawful under the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, including: content prohibited by the KSA Anti-Cybercrime Law, content offensive under KSA content regulations, or activity prohibited by the CST or CITC.

4.6.2 You must **not** upload, transmit, or submit through the platform: content that is defamatory, harassing, or threatening; content that infringes the intellectual property rights of any third party; content that violates the privacy of any individual; or content designed to deceive or defraud.

4.6.3 You must **not** use the platform to facilitate any form of bribery, money laundering, or corrupt activity as defined under KSA law.

## 5. Academy Operator Obligations

Academy operators using the Academy tier accept additional obligations:

5.1 You are responsible for ensuring that **all cadets provisioned under your Academy account** are made aware of this AUP and agree to comply with it before accessing the platform.

5.2 You must **not** provision seats for individuals who are not enrolled cadets or staff of your organisation.

5.3 You must ensure that use of the platform by your cadets complies with your own GACA-approved training programme documentation. Fly GACA is a study supplement, not a replacement for approved training materials or qualified instructors.

5.4 You are responsible for promptly notifying Fly GACA (support@flygaca.com) if you become aware of a cadet misusing the platform in breach of this AUP.

## 7. Enforcement

7.1 **Warning:** For a first or minor breach, Fly GACA may issue a warning with a requirement to cease the prohibited use.

7.2 **Suspension:** Fly GACA may suspend account access where a breach is ongoing, serious, or repeated, pending investigation.

7.3 **Termination:** Fly GACA may permanently terminate access for serious breaches, including (but not limited to): operational misuse that creates an aviation safety risk; impersonation of GACA; systematic scraping; and repeated AUP violations.

7.4 **Reporting:** Fly GACA reserves the right to report violations to relevant authorities, including GACA, CST/CITC, and law enforcement, where the violation may involve unlawful activity under KSA law.

7.5 **No refund:** Where an account is terminated for AUP breach, Fly GACA is not obliged to provide a refund of any prepaid subscription fees.

7.6 Enforcement actions are taken at Fly GACA's reasonable discretion. We will act proportionately and give the affected user an opportunity to respond before terminating an account, except where the breach creates an immediate safety or legal risk.
