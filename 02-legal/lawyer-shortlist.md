# Saudi IP Lawyer — Candidate Shortlist (P0-1 & P0-2)

**Purpose:** candidates to send the `lawyer-brief.md` to, for a written opinion on the
redistribution-rights question (P0-1) and the "Fly GACA" name question (P0-2).

**Date:** 23 May 2026

> This is research, not a recommendation or endorsement of any firm, and not legal
> advice. Firm details change — verify everything on each firm's own website before
> contacting them. Approach **two or three** firms, compare fee quotes, and choose.

---

## What to look for

The brief covers a narrow, specialised problem, so the firm matters. Prioritise:

- **Saudi-qualified**, with a real **copyright and trademark** practice (not only patents).
- **Current on the 2026 Copyright Law** — it was published in February 2026 and is not
  yet in force, so the firm must be actively tracking it and SAIP's implementing
  regulations.
- Comfortable giving a **written opinion** (some IP shops mainly do filing/prosecution
  work and are less suited to an advisory opinion).
- Able to quote a **fixed fee** — important for a solo founder.
- **No conflict** — the firm should not act for GACA or related government bodies on
  matters that would conflict. Ask this directly.
- A bonus: familiarity with **AI / technology** matters, given the Article-26 AI
  exception in question 1(g).

## Tier 1 — Saudi full-service firms with a strong IP practice

**AlGhazzawi & Partners (Ghazzawi Law Firm)** — `ghazzawilawfirm.com`
One of the longest-established Saudi firms, with offices in Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam and
a dedicated IP practice. Notably, AlGhazzawi & Partners **co-authored the leading
published analysis of the 2026 Copyright Law** with Gowling WLG — a strong signal they
are current on exactly the law in question 1. A good first call.

**Hammad & Al-Mehdar (HMCO)** — `hmco.com.sa`
Established Saudi firm with an IP and technology focus and published commentary on the
new IP regime. Likely comfortable with both the copyright and the AI-exception angles.

## Tier 2 — Dedicated Saudi IP firms (filing + advisory)

**Kadasa Intellectual Property** — `kadasa.com.sa`
One of the best-known dedicated IP firms in the Kingdom, handling copyright and trademark
work. Strong for the trademark question (P0-2); confirm they will also give a written
copyright opinion.

**Abu-Ghazaleh Intellectual Property (AGIP)** — `agip.com`
Large regional IP firm with a Riyadh office and very high trademark volume across the
Middle East. Efficient and well-resourced for the P0-2 trademark/registrability question;
confirm advisory (opinion) capability for P0-1.

**Al-Otaishan Intellectual Property & Technology (AIP&T)**
Saudi IP firm with a substantial trademark and design prosecution record and a stated
technology focus — the technology angle may suit question 1(g) on the AI exception.

**Almazroua Intellectual Property** — `almazroua-ip.com`
Saudi IP firm publishing on the Law of Trademarks and its implementing regulations —
relevant to the P0-2 registrability analysis.

## Tier 3 — International firms with Riyadh IP/AI practices

These will be the most expensive option, but they are deepest on the brand-new 2026 law
and the AI exception. Worth a quote if budget allows, or for a second opinion.

**Gowling WLG (Riyadh)** — `gowlingwlg.com`
Published the most detailed practitioner analysis of the 2026 Copyright Law (with
AlGhazzawi & Partners); has a Riyadh-based IP team. Strong on both the copyright and AI
angles.

**A&O Shearman** and **Reed Smith** — both have published on the new Copyright Law and
have Middle East IP capability. **Bird & Bird** maintains an AI regulatory tracker for
Saudi Arabia and is a good fit for the AI-exception question specifically.

## How to engage

1. **Shortlist 2–3** from Tier 1 / Tier 2 (a Saudi firm is likely the most
   cost-effective for a focused opinion; add one Tier 3 firm only if you want a
   higher-cost second opinion).
2. Send a **short intro email** plus the `lawyer-brief.md` (consider converting it to PDF
   first — see the note below).
3. Ask each firm, up front, for: a **fixed-fee quote** for the written opinion, an
   estimated **turnaround time**, and a **conflict check** (do they act for GACA?).
4. Confirm the lawyer who would handle it is **Saudi-qualified** and current on the 2026
   Copyright Law and SAIP practice.
5. Compare quotes and pick. Keep the engagement scoped to the two questions in the brief
   to control cost.

**Suggested intro line:** *"I'm the founder of an educational Saudi aviation platform
about to launch. I need a written opinion on two IP questions — whether we may rehost
official GACA regulations, and whether we can use the name 'Fly GACA'. A full brief is
attached. Could you provide a fixed-fee quote and confirm you have no conflict with
GACA?"*

> **Tip:** the brief is currently a Markdown file. If you'd like a polished PDF or Word
> version to attach to emails, that can be generated from `lawyer-brief.md` — just ask.
